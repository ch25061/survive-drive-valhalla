# Survive Drive — Valhalla

This repository's `main` branch is the **primary development baseline** for Survive Drive as of September 28, 2026. New Survive Drive features and fixes should start from this Valhalla version.

The current development Site is [survive-drive-valhalla.choppy777.chatgpt.site](https://survive-drive-valhalla.choppy777.chatgpt.site/) (`appgprj_6abaa02b28f0819189305df71487726c`). The existing HERE-based Site at [choppy.fans](https://choppy.fans) remains available at its current address. Moving the public main address is a separate decision.

Valhalla routing, Escape Gate cross-product searches, and route evaluation are implemented here. The Valhalla service is hosted locally on this computer; the Site needs that local connection for route searches. No traffic integration is planned yet.

## Working on this project

- Comparison shows the union of both routes' mapped cells: unknown cells are grey, known cells retain their value colors, shared cells are drawn once, and surrounding honeycomb outlines stay transparent. Saved known evidence for the exact same cell supplies its display value without changing candidate evidence or ranking. Both routes' cells support inspection and the cell-opacity control; ending comparison restores the active route's cell layer. `node check-route-comparison-cells.mjs` verifies these behaviors offline.
- In family display, every route card has a separate comparison checkbox. Check exactly two routes (including expanded members or different families) to compare them automatically on the map; unchecking either stops comparison. The map fits both saved paths, labels A in blue and B in vermilion, and highlights exact shared geometry with a thin, opaque purple line. Collinear shared portions are matched even with different vertex spacing; proximity and crossing points do not count. **Hide turn bubbles / Show turn bubbles** controls numbered maneuvers across redraws. **End comparison** unchecks both routes and restores the normal map. Comparison changes no rankings, inclusion, route choice, or provider authorization. `node check-route-comparison.mjs` checks geometry, map rendering, selection limits, turn visibility, and evidence preservation offline.
- The original ranked route list remains the default. **Show route families** switches to a compact display of similar paths; **Show ranked routes** restores the full list. Each family always displays its best-ranked route at the top. The selected route stays in its ranked position with a Selected marker; a collapsed family also identifies a selected member among its other routes. **Show full details** expands the top card's original gate and evidence details independently of **Show other routes**, which exposes the remaining routes and individual inclusion controls. Grouping is approximate and affects display only. Independent sliders set required mutual path overlap (50–100%, default 80%) and nearby path distance (0–500 metres, default 200 m), regrouping immediately. Matching direction, endpoint proximity, and relative-length checks remain in place. Missing or invalid geometry stays separate. `node check-route-families.mjs` verifies grouping, display switching, selection, filtering, and unchanged evidence state offline.
- Edit the source files in `dist/` and `server/`. `node build.mjs` copies browser assets into `dist/client/` and regenerates `dist/server/index.js` for publishing.
- New trips default to six Escape Gates at each end, producing 49 Start × Destination requests including no-gate choices. Saved explicit gate counts are preserved. Clicking a route fits its entire geometry in the visible map area. If the selected route already fits in the visible map, its view and dimensions stay unchanged. Only a clipped route resizes the map to the remaining viewport height and brings it into view if necessary; evidence refreshes preserve the current zoom.
- Each ranked route card shows Known Exclusion Zone Miles Under the independent Exclusion cutoff immediately after Known Red Miles. This is known non-highway distance strictly below that cutoff; unknown evidence stays unknown, and the extra statistic does not affect sorting.
- Keep the Escape Gate mechanism unless the user explicitly approves changing it. Preserve the full start/destination cross product, including the no-gate pair.
- Rank routes with known eligible AVM evidence by Known Red Miles Under the Red cutoff (fewest first), then Red Percent (lowest first), then Known Coverage (highest first). Returned travel time breaks remaining ties. Routes without known eligible evidence remain unranked and available for evidence selection. Miles without AVM evidence remain unknown and are not counted as Known Red Miles Under.
- Round 0 uses a configurable cull percentage (35% by default), rounded up from the original `N` candidates, and protects every route under 20% known coverage even above the nominal quota. Each completed evidence round removes that round's configured percentage, rounded up, from the previous nominal quota. With 27 routes and 35% throughout, the nominal quotas are 17, 11, then 7. Fill included slots from the full current ranking so excluded routes can return. A reranking without a completed round substitutes within the current quota; saved rounds retain their applied percentages.
- The current lookup workflow approves One Round at a time after an exact cost calculation. Route inclusion can be edited before preparing another round. Imported authorization records do not resume requests; multi-round paid authorization is retired.
- Run relevant offline checks before publishing. `check-ranked-route-selection.mjs`, `check-ranking-evolution.mjs`, `check-red-mileage-cull.mjs`, `check-map-avm-control-lock.mjs`, `check-adjustable-cull.mjs`, `check-route-quota.mjs`, and `check-pool-quota-server.mjs` cover ranking, coverage, culls, control restoration, and provider ceilings.
- Do not use live Valhalla, Census, or RentCast requests as routine tests. The explicitly requested `node check-valhalla-exclude-polygons.mjs --live` integration check is a narrow exception: it sends exactly two sequential requests to a loopback Valhalla `/route` endpoint (baseline, then the same trip with a polygon), has no retries, and sends nothing unless `--live` is present. Provider attempts, including failures, consume their stated budgets.
